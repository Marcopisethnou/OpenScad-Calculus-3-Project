# OpenScad-Calculus-3-Project

I volunteered over the summer of 2026 to help Professor Garrett Nelson, who is a Professor of Mathematics at Whitman College, with developing 3D printed models to help students learn more effectively in Calculus 3. We developed a total of 6 models that are currently used in Calculus 3 class for Fall 2026 semester.

I mainly helped with debugging OpenScad codes and also had independent learning opportunity to explore my own interests. The script files for printed models that are used in Calculus 3 will be uploaded as soon as I obtain permission from Professor Garrett Nelson.
